VERSION 0.3.0
Added 48 original synthetic portrait avatars, bundled for offline use.
Six regions: Asia, Europe, Africa, North America, South America and Oceania.
Each has four male and four female portraits; Mixed shows both.
All regions shows 48 built-in portraits, or 24 for a single gender.
No manual upload/tagging, network connection, API key or account is needed.
Portraits preview from a bundled atlas and insert as locally prepared PNG fills.
Upload controls are optional; existing custom photos and collections are preserved.
Keep your existing Figma plugin ID when upgrading. See README.txt.
